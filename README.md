# Opportunities-Portal

Status: Work in Progress

A skills-based career matching platform designed to connect students with employers through skill-based profiles and career opportunities.

The platform aims to help employers identify suitable candidates based on their skills and experience, while allowing students to identify skill gaps and connect with tutors to develop relevant competencies.

Planned Features:
Student profiles showcasing skills, proficiency levels and relevant projects.
Employer profiles and career opportunity listings.
Skills-based matching algorithm to rank candidates against job requirements.<img width="797" height="587" alt="Screenshot 2026-11-12 at 14 43 37" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/b7e26880-3ca3-48e4-8d97-47fa48aec3b9" />

Percentage-based compatibility scores displayed on the dashboard.
Peer-to-peer tutoring system to help students develop missing skills.

My Contributions:
Designed and implemented Firebase databases for student profiles and employer advertisements.
Worked on integrating database information with the platform dashboard.
Contributed to the development of the skills-based matching functionality.
Technologies:
Firebase
Gitlab
VsCode

This project is currently under development, and features are subject to change.
